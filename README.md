# AI Solution Design for a Business Problem

## Selected Domain
Manufacturing

## Business Problem
Manufacturing companies often face challenges in identifying defective products such as dents, scratches, and stains during quality inspection. Manual inspection processes can be slow, inconsistent, and expensive.

## Proposed AI Solution
The proposed solution uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) based image classification system to automatically detect defective products from manufacturing images.

## AI Task Type
Image Classification

## Recommended Model
Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

## Expected Business Impact
- Faster product inspection
- Reduced manual effort
- Improved product quality
- Lower operational costs
- Better customer satisfaction

## Responsible AI Considerations
- Risk of incorrect predictions
- Need for balanced training data
- Human oversight during quality checks
- Monitoring model performance regularly
